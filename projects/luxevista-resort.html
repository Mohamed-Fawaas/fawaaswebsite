Project Overview

LuxeVista Resort is a native Android (Java) guest app. Users register and log in against a local SQLite database, land on a dashboard and move through a complete booking journey: choose a room, confirm the booking, enter payment details (validated, simulated payment) and receive a thank-you screen. Extra screens cover service reservations, exclusive offers, nearby attractions and contact support.

Technologies & Languages Used

Java
Android
SQLite

Key Features

  • Register and login backed by SQLite (DatabaseHelper)
  • Dashboard with 5 guest services
  • Room booking: Ocean View Suite, Deluxe, Superior and Executive Suite
  • Booking confirmation passed to payment via Intent extras
  • Payment form validation: 16-digit card, MM/YY expiry, 3-digit CVV (simulated payment)
  • Spa, dining and cabana reservation screen
  • Offers, nearby attractions and contact-support screens

How It Works – Main Functions

1
DatabaseHelper.addUser / checkUser

Creates the users table and registers / verifies accounts in SQLite.

2
RoomBookingActivity

Shows the room options and sends the chosen room and price to the confirmation screen.

3
PaymentActivity

Validates card number, expiry and CVV before moving to the thank-you screen (no real payment is processed).

4
DashboardActivity

Central navigation to booking, services, offers, attractions and support.

5
ServiceReservationActivity

Handles spa, dining and cabana reservation actions.
