Project Overview

Guests order from a dynamic menu and follow an order reference; staff manage menu, payments and team in the admin studio; the kitchen display advances tickets. Prices are always calculated on the server.

Technologies & Languages Used

Node.js
Express
MySQL

Key Features

  • 12 sample dishes with filters and search
  • Basket with quantity controls and table checkout
  • Order tracking reference
  • Admin: revenue, trend, menu add/edit/archive, mark paid
  • Kitchen Display with role that can only advance tickets
  • Create/disable kitchen staff accounts, CSV export
