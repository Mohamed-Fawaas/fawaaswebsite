Project Overview

Users register, list surplus food with quantity, location and photo, and track their donations. Admins review, accept or decline, issue emergency food alerts and generate monthly category reports. Email updates are sent with PHPMailer.

Key Features

  • User & admin login, password reset by email
  • Donation form with image upload
  • My Donations history and status
  • Admin panel: accept/decline, emergency alerts
  • Monthly report by food category
  • Success stories, feedback and contact forms
